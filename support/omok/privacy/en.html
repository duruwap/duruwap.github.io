App Support/One-Color Gomoku

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-01

This policy explains how duruwap ("we") handles information in the mobile game "One-Color Gomoku" (the "App").

01Accounts and game records

The App has no sign-up or login. Settings (game rules, language, sound) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Games and win/loss records are not saved. Deleting the App also deletes the settings.

02Information handled by third parties

ServiceInformationPurpose
Google AdMobAdvertising identifier (IDFA / Advertising ID), approximate location from IP, device info, ad interactionsServing and measuring ads
Google Firebase AnalyticsApp instance ID, in-app usage events, device / OS / countryStatistics to improve the App
Google Firebase Cloud MessagingA delivery token (only if you turn notifications on)Delivering news and event notifications

These services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

03Your choices

  • Where consent is required (e.g. the EEA and UK), a consent form appears on first launch; you can change it any time under "Ad privacy settings" in the menu.
  • On iOS, the App asks for tracking permission after your first game. The game works the same if you decline.
  • You receive notifications only if you turn them on, and can turn them off in Settings at any time.
  • Usage statistics are not collected before consent is confirmed.

04Children

The App is not directed primarily at children under 13.
