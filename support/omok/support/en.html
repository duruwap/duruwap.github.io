App Support/One-Color Gomoku

Support

One-Color Gomoku · Gomoku with stones that turn to clear glass

One-Color Gomoku is gomoku where every stone turns to clear glass the moment it's placed. You can see where each stone is, but not whether it's black or white. Remember who played where and make five in a row first. When the game ends, every stone's color is revealed in the order it was played.

01FAQ

I can't see the colors of the stones. Is that a bug?

No, that's the rule of One-Color Gomoku. Every stone on the board appears as clear glass, and all colors are revealed when the game ends.

Some spots won't let me place a stone.

Black is not allowed to play a double-three (can be turned off in Settings) — a move that makes two open threes at once — and a notice is shown. If No overline for Black is on, Black also can't make six or more in a row. These checks use the stones' real colors.

How are the AI levels different?

Like people, the AI has a limited memory. Easy remembers the colors of only the last 6 moves and Normal the last 16, while Hard remembers every move and reads further ahead.

I have to tap twice to place a stone.

To prevent misplaced stones, the first tap shows a preview and a second tap on the same spot places it. You can switch to single tap in Settings.

Can I change the time limit per move?

Yes. In Settings, choose none, 10, 20, 30 or 60 seconds, and whether running out of time passes the turn or loses the game. The AI has no time limit.

How do I change my ad privacy settings?

In regions where consent is required (such as Europe), you can change it any time from Ad privacy settings in the menu. On iOS, you can also change tracking permission in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

How do I turn off notifications?

Turn them off in the app's settings, or in your device's Settings › Notifications.

The app freezes or behaves strangely.

Update to the latest version from the store, then fully close the app and open it again. If the problem continues, contact us with your app version and device details.
