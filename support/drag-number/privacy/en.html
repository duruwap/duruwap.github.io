02Information processed and purposes

① Information collected by the Developer: none

The App has no sign-up or login, and the Developer does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address or phone number. Game records (scores, games played, best score per mode) and settings (language, sound, background music) are stored only on your device and are never sent to the Developer or to any external server.

05Behavioral information collected by third parties (personalized ads)

In the App, Google LLC may directly collect behavioral information, such as advertising identifiers and ad usage history, through AdMob and use it to serve ads and personalized ads. Google processes this information under its own Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The Developer does not sell personal information to Google or provide it to Google in exchange for compensation.

How to opt out of personalized ads

  • iOS: Turn off tracking for the App in your device's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: Delete or reset your advertising ID in your device's Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads).
  • Regions where consent is required, such as Europe: Change it under Ad Privacy Settings in the App menu.

You can keep using the App if you opt out; non-personalized ads will be shown.

07Consent and choices

  • In regions where consent is required, such as Europe, a consent form appears on first launch, and you can change your choice at any time under "Ad Privacy Settings" in the menu.
  • On iOS, the App asks whether to allow tracking after your first game. You can keep using the App the same way if you do not allow it.
  • You receive notifications only if you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time in the App's settings or your device settings.
  • In regions where consent is required, usage statistics are not collected until consent is confirmed.

08Your rights and how to exercise them

You may at any time request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and withdrawal of consent regarding your personal information.

  1. Copy your ID from Settings › Analytics ID in the App.
  2. Send your Analytics ID and your request to duruwap@naver.com.
  3. The Developer will process your request and reply with the result within 10 days of receiving it. If it cannot be processed within that time, we will tell you the reason and the expected date.
  • You can delete records stored on your device yourself with "Reset Records" in Settings or by deleting the App.
  • You can opt out of ad-related information as described in Section 05, and Google account users can also manage it at https://myadcenter.google.com.
  • Because the Developer does not keep information that identifies you, we cannot locate a specific user's statistics data without the Analytics ID. Even in that case, the data is automatically deleted once the period in Section 03 has passed.
  • You may also exercise your rights through a legal representative or an authorized agent.

09Security measures

The Developer takes the following measures to protect personal information.

  • Minimal collection: There is no sign-up or login, and game records are stored only on your device.
  • Access control: Only the Developer can access the Google, Firebase and AdMob admin consoles, and two-step verification is used.
  • Protection in transit: Communication with Google services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Minimal retention: The retention period for statistics data is set to the shortest option (2 months).

10Children's personal information

The App is not directed primarily at children, and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 (Republic of Korea) or under 13 (United States and elsewhere). If we learn that such a child's information has been processed, we will delete it immediately. Parents or guardians can request deletion as described in Section 08.
