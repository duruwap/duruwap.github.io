App Support/Drag Number

Support

Drag Number · Draw a box, match the sum, pop! A number puzzle

Drag Number is a puzzle game: drag a box over the board, and if the numbers inside add up to the target number at the top, they pop. Clear as many tiles as you can before time runs out.

01FAQ

What's the difference between the modes?

Easy is 90 seconds on a smaller board (8×12), and Challenge is 120 seconds on a larger board (10×17). In Survival there's no time limit: a 10-second gauge drains, refills completely every time you clear tiles, and the game ends when it runs out.

Can a box include empty spaces?

Yes. Empty spaces don't count toward the sum, so only the remaining numbers need to add up to the target. This is handy for joining numbers that are far apart.

How do I reset my records?

Tap Reset Records in Settings. Deleting the app also removes the records stored on your device.

I want my data deleted.

You can delete the records stored on your device right away with Reset Records in Settings or by deleting the app. To also delete your app usage statistics, copy the Analytics ID from Settings and send it to duruwap@naver.com. We will delete the data within 10 days and let you know the result. For details, see Section 08 of the Privacy Policy.

How do I change my ad privacy settings?

In regions where consent is required (such as Europe), you can change it any time from Ad Privacy Settings in the menu. On iOS, you can also change tracking permission in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

How do I turn off notifications?

Turn them off in the app's settings, or in your device's Settings › Notifications.

The app freezes or behaves strangely.

Update to the latest version from the store, then fully close the app and open it again. If the problem continues, contact us with your app version and device details.
